# A face prompt: a demo person

**This is a demo person, not our presenter.** We wrote it to show the structure and the length of the prompt that fixes a face: a four-photo reference sheet, then the identity words you reuse in every later prompt. Our own presenter's prompt and sheets stay private, because she is the channel's face and we don't want anyone posing as us with her likeness.

The demo person is a fictional adult, androgynous, and not based on any real or famous person. Image Generator, `nano-banana-pro`, 3:4, 2K, no reference image, one image, 8 credits. We have not run this demo prompt.

## The prompt for the sheet that fixes the face
```
A character reference sheet made of four amateur phone photos of the same person, laid out on one white page in a 2x2 grid: top left a close-up of their face looking straight at the camera, top right a three-quarter view, bottom left a side profile, bottom right a full-body shot. Every photo is taken against the same plain white wall with an amateur phone camera in soft natural light: slight grain, a touch of overexposure on the wall, no studio lighting, no props, no dramatic shadows, very realistic low-fidelity phone snapshots. They are an androgynous adult in their early thirties with a calm, creative look: short, tousled, dark-brown hair with a shaved undercut and a slightly longer fringe swept to one side; a square face with a strong, soft-edged jaw, tan skin with visible pores and a few sun lines at the eyes, deep-set grey-green eyes with no make-up, thick straight dark eyebrows with a small gap in the left one, a broad nose with a slight bend, and a small silver stud in the right nostril. A relaxed, slightly amused expression and a direct gaze. They wear a loose heather-grey crew-neck sweatshirt, straight dark-olive work trousers and worn tan leather boots, a thin black cord bracelet. Same face, hair, outfit and accessories in all four photos. No text, no labels, no logos.
```

## The identity words you reuse in every later prompt
```
short tousled dark-brown hair with a shaved undercut and a longer fringe swept to one side, a square face with a soft-edged jaw, tan skin with visible pores and sun lines at the eyes, deep-set grey-green eyes, thick straight dark eyebrows with a small gap in the left one, a broad nose with a slight bend, a small silver stud in the right nostril
```
Describe one real feature at a time, put the marks (here the eyebrow gap and the nostril stud) in the same words every time, and say which side of the picture they are on. Check each render for the mirror flip, as we did.

Fill the slot `[your character's identity words: hair, face, marks, make-up, jewellery]` in the other prompts of this repository with words like these.
