# 03 · Podcast frames, voice-over, lip-sync, product ad

The "podcast" and "product ad" sequences. PhotoGenerator.ai tools used: Image Generator (`nano-banana-pro`), Image Edit, Create Voice-over (a library voice; its name is left out of this repo on purpose), Lip-Sync (`kling-avatar-v2-pro`) and Frame to Video (Seedance 2.0). Credit prices are what the button showed on 30 and 31 October 2026 and may change.

The character is the fictional character from files 01 and 02. Her lines are written by us.
