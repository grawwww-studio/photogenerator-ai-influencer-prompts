# 01 · Character and character sheet

The first identity we built for the fictional influencer; the second one, the presenter's, is not shared (see section B). Everything here was typed into PhotoGenerator.ai's web app (Image Generator, `nano-banana-pro`, 2K, one image per click, 8 credits each unless noted). The character is fictional.

Every prompt below is the text we typed before the click, with only the character's surname removed. Where we retried, both versions are shown.

## B. The identity in the video: not shared

The second identity is our channel's presenter. Her prompt and her character sheets stay private, because she is the channel's face and we don't want anyone posing as us with her likeness. The method is all here: `presenter/FACE_PROMPT.md` is a demo prompt of the same structure and length for a different, fictional person, and `presenter/SHEET_TEMPLATE.md` shows how the sheets are built.

Every later prompt in this repository that needs her identity carries the slot `[your character's identity words: hair, face, marks, make-up, jewellery]` where her words were. Fill it with your own character's words.
