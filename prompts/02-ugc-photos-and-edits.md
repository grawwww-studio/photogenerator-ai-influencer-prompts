# 02 · UGC-style photos, retries and edits

Photos of the second identity (the `@image1` sheet from file 01, section B2) made to look like a real feed: an unedited phone photo, one physical light source, at most two imperfection cues, no polish words. Image Generator, `nano-banana-pro`, 2K, one image, 8 credits each; the aspect ratio is in the heading. In the app you insert the sheet with its @ picker.

## Session 2 (planned shots, run one by one)

Each prompt was written before the click. After the first results we added short lock lines (named in each heading) to fix what had gone wrong in the earlier shots: a phone inside the picture, a second phone in a reflection, readable text on signs. In these prompts `@Character` stands for the character and the sheet is the second reference; in Image Generator the character chip is not offered, so we used the sheet as `@image1` (see file 03).
